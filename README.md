Amazon Prime – Churn & Retention Analysis Dashboard

📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn and retention patterns for Amazon Prime using Power BI. The dashboard provides an interactive view of customer behavior, churn rate, retention rate, customer lifetime value, tenure, subscription plans, contract types, payment methods, and acquisition channels.

The objective is to identify important factors contributing to customer churn and generate insights that can help improve customer retention and subscription performance.

---

🎯 Objectives

- Analyze the overall customer churn rate.
- Measure customer retention performance.
- Compare churn rates across different regions.
- Analyze churn based on subscription plans.
- Study the relationship between contract type and churn.
- Analyze payment methods and total customer charges.
- Compare churn across customer acquisition channels.
- Analyze Average Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).
- Understand average customer tenure.
- Identify patterns that can help improve customer retention.

---

📈 Dashboard Analysis

1. Churn Rate by Region

The dashboard compares customer churn across different regions:

- Central
- East
- North
- South
- West

This helps identify regions with relatively higher or lower customer churn.

2. Churn Rate by Subscription Plan

Customer churn is analyzed across:

- Basic
- Premium
- Standard

This analysis helps understand whether subscription plans have an impact on customer retention.

3. Churn Rate by Contract Type

The dashboard compares:

- Month-to-Month
- One Year
- Two Year

Contract type analysis can help identify customer groups that are more likely to discontinue their subscriptions.

4. Total Charges by Payment Method

Customer charges are analyzed according to payment methods such as:

- Debit Card
- UPI
- Credit Card
- Net Banking
- PayPal

This provides an understanding of payment-method-wise customer revenue contribution.

5. Churn Rate by Acquisition Channel

Customer churn is compared across acquisition channels including:

- Social Media
- Organic Search
- Referral
- Direct
- Unknown
- Paid Ads

This helps evaluate customer quality and retention across different acquisition sources.

6. Customer Value and Tenure

The dashboard also compares:

- Average Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Average Monthly Charges
- Average Customer Tenure

This provides an overview of customer value and engagement over time.

---

💡 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard:

- The dataset contains approximately 1,000 customers.
- The overall churn rate is 44%, while the retention rate is 56%.
- The average customer tenure is approximately 16.30 months.
- Churn can be compared across regions to identify areas requiring additional retention strategies.
- Subscription plans and contract types provide useful customer-segmentation dimensions for churn analysis.
- Acquisition channels show differences in customer churn behavior.
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), monthly charges, and tenure can be used together to identify high-value customers.
- Payment-method analysis provides additional insight into customer revenue patterns.

---

🔍 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following strategies can help improve customer retention:

1. Focus on high-churn customer segments
   Identify customers with a higher probability of leaving and provide targeted retention offers.

2. Improve month-to-month customer retention
   Encourage short-term subscribers to move toward longer-term subscription plans through discounts and loyalty benefits.

3. Strengthen customer engagement
   Personalized content recommendations and exclusive Prime benefits can improve customer engagement.

4. Target high-value customers
   Customers with high CLV should receive personalized offers and loyalty incentives.

5. Optimize acquisition channels
   Focus marketing efforts on channels that bring customers with better retention rates.

6. Monitor regional churn
   Regions with comparatively higher churn should be analyzed further to understand customer-specific issues.
